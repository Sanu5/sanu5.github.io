Delete your Mehfil account

Applies to the Mehfil apps for iOS and Android

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Mehfil and go to Profile → Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm. You will be asked to sign in again with Apple or Google to prove it is you.

This permanently deletes your account and every record in it — events, clients, crew, inventory, payments, expenses, enquiries and change requests — from our servers and revokes your Sign in with Apple token. It cannot be undone.

By email

If you can no longer open the app, email biswalworkanish@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject Delete my Mehfil account. We delete the account and all its data within 7 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted, what is kept

Everything listed above is deleted. Nothing is retained after deletion except our email exchange, if you contacted us. If you only want to clear your business records but keep the account, use Profile → Account → Erase all data instead.
