Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Mehfil is an operations app for wedding-service vendors. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

What we collect

  • Account details — when you sign in with Apple or Google we receive your name and email address (Apple may provide a private relay address). These identify your account and are shown in Profile → Account.
  • Business data you enter — events, clients and their phone numbers, crew, inventory, payments, expenses, enquiries and change requests. This is your data; Mehfil stores it so it is available on your devices.
  • App settings — appearance preference and an unsaved event draft, stored only on your device.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or analytics, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Where it is stored

Business data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase) in the asia-south1 region, encrypted in transit and at rest, under an account only you can access. Security rules restrict every record to the signed-in owner. A copy is cached on your device so the app works offline.

How it is used

Only to provide the app's features. We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising.

Third parties

Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google LLC). Sign in with Apple (Apple Inc.) and Google Sign-In (Google LLC) handle authentication under their own privacy policies. WhatsApp, SMS and phone links open the respective apps on your device; Mehfil does not send messages itself.

Your choices

  • Export — every record is visible in the app; contact us for a full export.
  • Erase — Profile → Account → Erase all data removes your business records and keeps the account.
  • Delete — Profile → Account → Delete account removes your account and every record permanently, and revokes the Sign in with Apple token. This cannot be undone.

Children

Mehfil is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.
