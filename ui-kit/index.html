What makes it different

Most kits give you screens. This one carries a thread: a crew is double-booked on 14 November. It surfaces on the home screen, becomes visible in the day timeline's overlapping lanes, is resolved on the crew-assignment screen — where the app shows what each choice will cost before you make it — and lands four screens later as a negative margin on the expenses screen. Six screens, one consequence.
